# Rounded joins between retained original junction arcs

`retained_arc_fillets.reconcile` repairs reflex switches between two finite
exposed original circular junction curbs. It adds an extensive local pavement
wedge and a tangent auxiliary circle. The two original circles, their radius
modes and their exact finite parent parameters remain explicit boundary
primitives. No Approach, source End, Street, ownership claim or logical Corner
is invented for an auxiliary arc. Generated area records are typed and do not
become original incoming contact constraints.

An original prepared curb can contain a true circle and a negative auxiliary
lead. The corner producer records the exact prepared-parameter to circle-phase
knots, original Corner identity and separate lead domains while appending the
unchanged geometry. The retained wrapper evaluates only that declared circle
portion analytically. Existing leads and their prepared knots remain intact;
contacts convert through the explicit map. It does not infer a circle domain
from nearby points or treat the complete lead-and-arc phase as a circle. A
missing or inconsistent domain remains a refusal. Existing pure-circle views
retain their previous parameter arithmetic and geometry exactly.

The ordinary preferred STYLE candidate sequence is tried first. A bounded
auxiliary tail continues the same reduction down to the existing 0.5 m rounded
gore floor. This is a local curb auxiliary radius, not a replacement of the
original vehicular corner radius. A participating STRICT minimum is retained:
a smaller auxiliary join declines rather than silently overriding it. At most
16 pairs, two closure batches and the existing source, segment and mesh work
budgets are permitted.

Several joins can consume different parts of the same original circle. Their
finite consumed intervals are reserved by exact parent object, owner and curve
index. A candidate that would overlap another reservation, or leave only a
collapsed parent interval, tries the next existing STYLE radius. Future STRICT
joins reserve their sole allowed candidate first, while final publication keeps
the original pair order and owner keys. An unavailable hard candidate remains
fatal. This does not change the 17-attempt limit or add a smaller hard radius.
Disjoint existing successes retain their geometry and metadata.

Every accepted contact lies inside an explicitly exposed finite parent arc.
The normal canonical sampler first uses each original prepared chord budget.
At an exact singular tangency, the explicit parent contact parameters may
hand off to the new primitive; both original chord budgets and finite ancestry
are rechecked. There is no nearest-curve source assignment. The complete new
arc must remain exposed, all internal closure edges must remain hidden, and
no genuine large reflex source switch may remain.

Later redistribution keeps the stricter of the caller's curve tolerance and
the fillet's recorded prepared chord budget, together with every existing
source-reference knot inside the finite requested interval. It leaves the
analytical circle, radius, contacts and source points unchanged. A coarse
caller therefore cannot discard a finer source certificate before another
physical union. Forward and reversed requests use the same bounded sample
set; the existing allocation still rejects excessive mandatory knots. An
already aligned native sample set remains exact. This also prevents an
unchanged original area union from appearing to enlarge a hole merely because
an earlier retained-circle rim used coarser chords.

At a shallow internal circle tangency, prepared parent chords can create a
short disconnected fillet fragment. A bounded canonicalization recognizes
only that explicit parent object, owner and curve index; its re-entry spans
must lie entirely inside the recorded consumed parent interval. The exact
circle tangency and both original chord budgets are checked at both contacts.
The intended full fillet then replaces that alternating derived run. A third
source, a real finite endpoint or an unsupported contact declines. The normal
sampler still permits each of the at most 32 contact tokens only once, and all
source-width, complete-mouth, hole and prepared-neighbor proofs run afterward.

Prepared chords can also hide a whole shallow fillet. In that case, both
recorded original chord contacts must identify a unique two-parent boundary
chain entirely inside the declared consumed intervals. The analytical arc
must stay within both original parent chord budgets. Every unrelated physical
operand must be continuously separated from it. An inconclusive nearby whole
source strip can use its complete original native grid for bounded proof-only
refinement; these cells never become area operands or new ownership. The
shared refinement allocation is 4096 points across the existing fillet family.
A covered third source, invalid interval or exhausted allocation declines.
The normal sampler must subsequently expose every entire fillet and rerun all
source, profile, hole and eventual prepared-neighbor checks.

An original logical corner can reach a later physical contact through the
existing typed, wholly owned source-road walk. Its original junction incidence,
cyclic sector and radius policy are validated separately from the actual
contact profiles and finite source prefixes. This narrow authority check does
not make the displaced contact an original End at the logical junction.

The original incoming mouths and profiles stay exact. The final physical
union retains every original area operand, the same positive hole topology,
and fresh continuous 2 mm containment proofs for consumed strips and all
claimed finite prefixes. Only the consumed junction intervals of the original
circles change exposure; all other original circle portions remain unchanged.

Source-height conditions derive from the explicit original parents. A closed
homogeneous zero curb remains zero, and a genuine homogeneous inner plateau
retains its original peak. Mixed generated fragments blend between surviving
real source-bank and incoming-profile anchors; their provisional crossing
heights do not become authored source constraints. Complete source heights,
body crowns, stored mesh quality, slopes and drainage require separate Product
and native qualification. The seven-arc Beijing 185 prototype demonstrates
these local arc guards; it does not qualify the distinct source-bank connector
closure or complete city runtime.
