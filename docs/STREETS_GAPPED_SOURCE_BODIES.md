# Original source bodies around separated side junctions

`gapped_source_plan.GappedSourcePlan.build(context, bundle)` is an explicit
union-plan family for two complete original members with real, separated
one-sided intermediate junctions. Existing successful Bundle providers remain
first. Ineligible source arrangements raise `GappedSourceUnavailable`; failed
eligible geometry remains an error. The implementation contains no city IDs.

`GappedSourceInventory` partitions original ordered visits at actual three-arm
incidences. `physical_source_spans` gives every boundary its original physical
End; hydration may resolve an already published two-alias merged mouth, but
creates no End or alias. A Section wholly inside one actual compound remains
query-owned only after its complete original width passes the independent
2 mm source-strip certificate. Original Path objects, member traversal,
finite stations and the common source-sampling phase remain authoritative.

A moved ordinary mouth can also consume a complete first or last original
Section before exposing its profile on the next Section of the same real
Route. Hydration retains `original_visits` and its certified
`owned_source_intervals`. The Gapped ledger checks their original ordering,
Path identity, real owner, exact finite cut and complete-width certificate.
Both whole and partial routed prefixes enter the extent proof without becoming
body visits or new shared claims. Missing, repeated or mismatched prefix
records reject; forward and reversed source fixtures cover this contract.

Each separated gap retains both complete near-side profiles and the actual
published inner curb between them. Its opposite source interval must be one
continuous original span with strictly positive separation over the entire
gap and its finite entrance bridges. At most eight ordered, nonoverlapping
gaps are supported. An outer field connector across an owned junction is
labelled computational and is never meshed as pavement.

`GappedSourceBodyPiece` reuses source-preserving profile bridges and finite
`MergedRunBody` cores outside the junction intervals. Across each junction,
only the opposite original carriageway band is body-owned. The new grass disk
uses the exact near-side junction curb, both source-bridge inner rims, the
opposite original inner bank and the cores' shared whole grass cuts. Private
common fibres are evaluation/mesh interfaces; they do not become source ports
or replace the global original-End registry. Outer rounded caps retain actual
junction and unconsumed crossroad curb samples. A crossroad curb also receives
a normal carriageway whole-edge and overlap audit.

The source phase and authored curve tolerance do not change. This new
consumer gives existing chart helpers a local construction tolerance no larger
than 1.5 mm. Its report records both tolerances. This smaller chord allowance
leaves room for the unchanged independent 2 mm complete-width test, including
its separately deducted source chord error. The final proof covers every
visible original interval and every wholly query-owned Section. The station
ledger additionally covers the accepted original junction prefixes; it does
not mislabel those original junction footprints as rectangular road strips.

Acceptance requires exact complete physical profiles, identical whole grass
interfaces to new or existing pavement, manifold boundaries, positive physical
and stored quads, edge ratio at most 8, pavement sine at least 0.03, unchanged
physical/longitudinal slope bounds, zero interior drainage fill, finite UVs and
independent overlap checks against the actual query-owner meshes. Grass keeps
its established positive-corner/ratio-8 contract. Each old core retains its
8,000-cell budget. A long core can use two individually bounded children with
identical whole profile/grass cuts; their combined leaf budget is 16,000 cells.
The assembled family retains its explicit 50,000-cell cap.

The plan publishes only the new body and grass. Existing junction pavement and
grass enter its physical height query without being republished. All original
member Section IDs remain in `source_ids`. Complete station coverage uses
`CarriagewayProvider.span_metadata`; it does not require a road task or create
a Bundle-plan/road dependency cycle. Physical queries are retained when display
geometry is released and remain valid at zero exaggeration.

The captured current Bergamo Bundle10 qualifies 5,319 new pavement quads and
1,120 grass quads around its real separated side junction. All ten original
Sections remain in the ownership ledger, six physical profile interfaces are
exact, and 444 whole grass edges match new or query-owned pavement. Stored
pavement ratio/sine are 7.99355/0.05068 and grass ratio is 7.69802. Continuous
2 mm width, slope, drainage, UV and independent overlap audits pass. The normal
plan takes about 47 seconds with cached actual junction products and with all
road/Bundle-plan products removed. This is a focused normal-plan qualification;
it does not establish complete-city readiness or interactive latency.

## Runtime admission and preservation of separated results

The normal provider tries the established Bundle families first. An existing
`separated` mode is a valid policy, so side-junction presence alone does not
authorize replacing it. Runtime admission additionally requires a substantial
overlap of the two **visible original carriageways**. Fully owned junction
spans, gaps and incoming owned prefixes cannot supply this witness.

`gapped_runtime_admission.py` retains each original visible station interval.
It bounds a whole transverse fibre using both endpoints of each source profile
cut. The sampled field only proposes candidate stations. Bounded monotone
inversion then evaluates all four **original analytical offset curbs** on the
same fibre, strictly within those source intervals. Their true strip overlap
must exceed both authored source-curve allowances plus the numeric margin.
Failure to find this witness leaves the established separated policy in place;
it does not itself certify separation.

Known typed unsupported inventory/frame/curb-chain cases can decline during
runtime discovery. Arbitrary source errors and all coverage, geometry and mesh
failures after admission remain failures. The explicit diagnostic API preserves
its original exceptions. A normal separated side-junction regression compares
all old/new mesh coordinates, topology, UVs and materials exactly.

The current Beijing overlapping case qualifies 28,046 new pavement and 6,076
grass quads, retaining two real grass holes and all seven original Sections.
Its original analytical admission witness has 546.6 mm overlap, versus the
combined 10 mm authored allowance; the Bergamo case has 855.3 mm. Continuous
two-millimeter source containment, stored shape/slopes, exact profiles, whole
grass interfaces, height queries at zero and exaggerated heights, UVs, buffer
release and changed-part overlap checks pass. These focused results do not
replace fresh complete-city qualification.
