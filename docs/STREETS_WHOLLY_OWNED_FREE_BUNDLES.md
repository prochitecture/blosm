# A wholly owned member beside a genuinely free counterpart

This separator family requires an explicit preparation certificate. It does
not drop an unsupported Bundle or infer an opposite junction owner.

One complete original member must be wholly claimed by the current compound;
both of its real terminal incidences must already belong to that owner. The
other complete original member must have two genuinely free ends: no authored
incidence, scope cut or selection cut. The existing original-centerline
separation certificate remains mandatory.

Preparation certifies every consumed source strip and incoming prefix within
the existing continuous 2 mm width allowance. It then proves that the free
counterpart's entire original full-width ribbon lies outside the owner's
outer region, including its grass-hole area. A disconnected road inside an
owned grass hole is therefore ineligible even when the pavements are disjoint.
The finite proof accounts for source chord error and the existing authored
curve tolerance; it changes no source geometry or numerical welding epsilon.

The separator uses one continuous existing published owner-curb bank and the
free Road's actual published inner bank. A bank interval must identify the
same original Path and offset, or an independently certified full source-strip
boundary. A free-cap plane must have a unique original crossing. Its private
grass closure selects the first existing bank vertex inside that plane,
within one existing edge and the ordinary spacing bound. All original bank
vertices and whole edges survive; no pavement or curb refinement is used.

Uniqueness counts crossings in both bank directions and rejects a positive
bank interval coincident with the free plane. An ordinary source group without
a prepared physical layout reports typed unavailability, preserving the
provider's original unsupported-visibility failure.

The counterpart remains an independent Road, and the compound owns only its
claimed sources. No End, Section, source alias or global port binding is
invented. The normal provider first tries its established algorithm. Only its
typed unequal-visibility failure can select this family; an unavailable
certificate preserves that original failure, while a failed eligible proof
remains fatal.

Publication retains the established grass contract: positive physical and
stored corner Jacobians, edge ratio at most 8, finite UVs, exact bank heights,
the unchanged grass slope limit, no drainage fill and full overlap checks
against the owner's pavement/grass and the counterpart's pavement. A sharp
fixed grass tip is reported without imposing the pavement-only 0.03 sine
criterion on grass.

The certificate and separator record their actual source intervals, widths,
centerline proof, bank provenance, free-cap choices and qualification results.
The owner signature depends explicitly on the counterpart's original Path and
Profile digests, so edits force fresh certification without broad mesh-product
dependencies.
